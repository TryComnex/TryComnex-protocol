# Comnex Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Most people already say what they want to do with their money in public: on X. Comnex closes the gap between saying it and doing it. The mission is simple: a post is the interface, Solana is the settlement layer, and the result is visible to anyone who cares to look.

We believe tools should meet people where they already are. No new dashboard, no extra window, no habit to unlearn.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Brand, site, public channels, live market board, and the address registry that keeps every official fact in one place.

**Phase 2: Launch.** Contract address announced, token row goes live on the board, community channels open.

**Phase 3: Commands.** The first set of post-to-transaction commands, shaped for plain language and visible results.

**Phase 4: Expansion.** More command types, tooling for builders, and deeper integrations across the Solana ecosystem.
